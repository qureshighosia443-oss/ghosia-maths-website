Online Diagnostic Assessment
A structured mathematics assessment designed to identify strengths, gaps and the level of support a student needs.

Before you begin

Choose the level that best matches the student's current programme. Each attempt creates a fresh randomized paper from the question bank.

Assessment format: 30 questions — 10 Basic, 10 Medium, 10 Harder. Time allowed: 45 minutes. The paper is randomized for each attempt.

Assessment rules

  • Work independently. No search engines, calculators unless specifically allowed by your teacher, or help from another person.
  • Stay on the assessment page. Leaving the page or switching tabs/windows is treated as an integrity violation.
  • First tab/fullscreen violation gives a warning. A second violation automatically submits the assessment.
  • Do not refresh the page during the test.
  • When time reaches zero, the assessment is submitted automatically.

Note: browser-based anti-cheating checks reduce opportunities but cannot detect every form of outside help or use of another device.
